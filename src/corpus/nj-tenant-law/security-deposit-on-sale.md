# Security Deposit on Sale or Transfer

## Transfer to new owner

When a rental property is **sold or transferred**, the seller must, within five business days of transfer, turn the security deposits (with accrued interest) over to the buyer/new owner. **NJ Stat 46:8-20** governs this transfer.

The new owner is then responsible for the deposits and assumes the same statutory duties.

The seller must also notify the tenant in writing of the transfer, identifying the new holder of the deposit.

## Tenant remedies on improper transfer

If the seller fails to transfer the deposits properly, the tenant may sue both the seller and the buyer. Both can be jointly liable for the tenant's deposit.

## Common red flags

- A lease assigned to a new landlord without written notice to the tenant of the new deposit holder.
- A new owner who claims they "didn't receive the deposit" from the seller — under §46:8-20 they are still on the hook to the tenant.
- A clause in a lease addendum that purports to "release" the original landlord from deposit liability after a sale; the release does not bind the tenant.
