RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT — STATE OF NEW JERSEY

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of July 1, 2026, between Maplewood Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Daniel Reyes ("Tenant"), for the leased premises at 412 Park Avenue, Apartment 2A, Newark, NJ 07104 ("Premises").

1. TERM. The lease term is twelve (12) months, beginning July 1, 2026, and ending June 30, 2027. Upon expiration, this Lease shall automatically convert to a month-to-month tenancy on the same terms unless either party provides the other with written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the current term.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay rent of two thousand two hundred dollars ($2,200.00) per month, due on the first day of each month. Rent may be paid by check, money order, or electronic transfer to Landlord at the address listed above.

3. LATE FEE. Rent is considered late if not received within five (5) business days of the due date (the "grace period"). After the grace period, Tenant shall pay a late fee equal to five percent (5%) of the monthly rent, assessed once per late payment. No additional per-day charges or compounding fees apply.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall provide Landlord with a security deposit in the amount of three thousand three hundred dollars ($3,300.00) — equal to one and one-half (1.5) months rent — consistent with N.J.S.A. 46:8-19. The deposit shall be held in a separate interest-bearing account at a New Jersey banking institution; interest earned, less Landlord's administrative fee permitted by statute, shall be credited annually to Tenant. The deposit shall be returned, with itemized deductions if any, within thirty (30) days after Tenant vacates the Premises, in accordance with N.J.S.A. 46:8-21.1.

5. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may terminate this Lease before the end of the term upon sixty (60) days written notice and payment of an early termination fee equal to one (1) month's rent. In addition, Tenant may terminate this Lease without penalty in any circumstance protected by New Jersey law, including but not limited to active military deployment (SCRA), domestic violence (N.J.S.A. 46:8-9.6 et seq.), or where Tenant is a senior citizen or disabled person relocating to a hospital, nursing home, or assisted-living facility (N.J.S.A. 2A:18-61.1).

6. SUBLETTING. Tenant may sublet or assign this Lease with Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. A request to sublet shall be answered in writing within fifteen (15) business days; failure to respond shall be deemed consent. Tenant remains primarily liable for rent unless Landlord releases Tenant in writing.

7. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE. Landlord shall maintain the Premises in habitable condition, consistent with the implied warranty of habitability under Marini v. Ireland, 56 N.J. 130 (1970), and shall make all repairs necessary to keep the Premises safe, sanitary, and fit for residential use. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any condition requiring repair. If Landlord fails to make a necessary repair within a reasonable time after written notice, Tenant may pursue repair-and-deduct or other remedies permitted under New Jersey law. Tenant is responsible for minor repairs caused by Tenant's negligence.

8. LANDLORD ENTRY. Landlord shall provide Tenant with at least twenty-four (24) hours written notice before entering the Premises for inspections, repairs, or to show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, and shall enter only at reasonable times. In the case of a genuine emergency (fire, flood, gas leak, or imminent threat to safety), Landlord may enter without notice.

9. PETS. Tenant may keep up to two (2) household pets (dogs or cats up to fifty (50) pounds each) upon written notice to Landlord and payment of a refundable pet deposit not to exceed one-half (0.5) months rent. Service animals and emotional support animals required as a reasonable accommodation under the Fair Housing Act and New Jersey Law Against Discrimination are permitted without pet deposit or additional rent.

10. ATTORNEYS FEES. In any action arising under or related to this Lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys fees and court costs. This provision applies reciprocally to Landlord and Tenant.

11. INDEMNIFICATION. Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Landlord from claims, liabilities, damages, and expenses arising from Tenant's negligent or intentional acts, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord, Landlord's employees, or Landlord's agents.

12. RETALIATION. Landlord shall not terminate this Lease, refuse to renew, raise rent, or otherwise retaliate against Tenant for any complaint to a governmental authority, request for repair, or exercise of any right under this Lease or New Jersey law, in accordance with N.J.S.A. 2A:42-10.10.

13. PARKING. Tenant is assigned one (1) parking space, designated by Landlord, at no additional cost. Landlord shall provide at least thirty (30) days written notice before relocating Tenant's parking. Parking may not be revoked except for documented cause.

14. JURY TRIAL. The parties retain their constitutional right to trial by jury in any action arising under or related to this Lease, except in proceedings statutorily designated as bench trials.

15. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey. The parties acknowledge that this Lease has been executed in New Jersey and the Premises are located in New Jersey.

By signing below, Tenant acknowledges receipt of a copy of this Lease and agrees to be bound by all of its terms.

LANDLORD: Maplewood Properties LLC, by Erin Caldwell, Managing Member

TENANT: Daniel Reyes
