# Automatic Lease Renewal — NJSA 46:8-10 (Continuation of Tenancy)

## Default to month-to-month at lease end under NJSA 46:8-10

Under **NJSA 46:8-10** (continuation of tenancy after expiration of term),
where a fixed-term residential lease expires and the tenant remains in
possession, the tenancy in NJ defaults to a **month-to-month tenancy** on the
same terms (rent, rules) as the prior lease, unless the lease provides
otherwise. **NJSA 46:8-10** sets the statutory baseline.

This default coexists with the Anti-Eviction Act (see anti-eviction-act): the
landlord may not evict the tenant simply because the fixed term has ended.
**NJSA 46:8-10** plus the Anti-Eviction Act together cabin landlord power over
the post-term tenancy.

## Express auto-renewal clauses

Some leases include an "automatic renewal" clause that purports to lock the
tenant into another full fixed term unless the tenant provides advance notice
(often 60 or 90 days). NJ courts scrutinize these against the **NJSA 46:8-10**
default — a clause that strips the tenant of the month-to-month statutory
fallback bears a heavy burden:

- The notice requirement must be conspicuous, not buried in fine print.
- The lease must give the tenant a meaningful opportunity to opt out (typically 30+ days from a clear reminder).
- The renewal terms must be the same as the original — a clause that triggers a rent increase upon auto-renewal is more vulnerable.

## Tenant-favorable opt-out norms

The cleaner practice — and the one most landlords use — is to send the tenant a written renewal offer 60–90 days before the term ends, requesting affirmative renewal rather than relying on an auto-renewal trap.

## Common red flags

- An auto-renewal clause that requires tenant notice **earlier than 60 days** before lease end — likely unreasonable against the **NJSA 46:8-10** default.
- An auto-renewal clause that triggers a rent increase the tenant did not affirmatively accept — strips the tenant of the **NJSA 46:8-10** month-to-month fallback.
- An auto-renewal clause that purports to lock the tenant into a 12+ month renewal term with no termination right — directly inverts the **NJSA 46:8-10** baseline.
- A clause that requires tenant notice via certified mail to a specific PO Box but the landlord did not provide a clear reminder.
