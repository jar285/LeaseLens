# Parking and Storage Clauses

## Generally lease-controlled

Parking, storage units, and shared-amenity access in NJ residential leases are governed by the lease itself. There is no specific NJ statute setting parking-fee caps or guaranteeing parking access.

## Implied terms

Where the lease describes parking as included with the unit, the landlord cannot unilaterally revoke or relocate parking during the lease term without the tenant's consent. A clause reserving the right to "modify parking arrangements at any time" may be enforced for reasonable changes (e.g., reassigning a numbered spot for resurfacing) but not to strip the tenant of access entirely.

## Anti-Eviction Act overlay

For Anti-Eviction-Act-covered units, a landlord cannot use parking revocation or storage-fee escalation as a back-door eviction tool. A material reduction in services to coerce a tenant to leave is itself a basis for a tenant claim.

## ADA / fair-housing accommodations

A tenant with a disability may request a **reasonable accommodation** for accessible parking — even if the lease does not provide a designated accessible spot. The landlord must engage in an interactive process and provide the accommodation unless it imposes an undue burden.

## Common red flags

- A clause allowing the landlord to revoke or relocate parking at any time with no notice and no rent reduction.
- Storage fees that compound or escalate sharply during the lease term beyond what the original lease disclosed.
- A clause that purports to deny accessible-parking accommodations — likely a fair-housing violation.
- A clause that imposes a parking fee not disclosed in the original lease and not subject to the same notice rules as rent changes.
