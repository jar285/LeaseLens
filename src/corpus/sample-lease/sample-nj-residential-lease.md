RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT — STATE OF NEW JERSEY

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of June 1, 2026, between Hudson Realty LLC ("Landlord") and Maya Chen ("Tenant"), for the leased premises at 245 Grove Street, Apartment 4B, Jersey City, NJ 07302 ("Premises").

1. TERM. The lease term is twelve (12) months, beginning June 1, 2026, and ending May 31, 2027. Upon expiration, this Lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless Tenant provides Landlord with written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the current term.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay rent of two thousand four hundred dollars ($2,400.00) per month, due on the first day of each month. Rent shall be paid by check or money order to Landlord at the address listed above.

3. LATE FEE. Any rent payment received after the due date shall incur a late fee of one hundred fifty dollars ($150.00) plus an additional ten dollars ($10.00) per day until the rent is paid in full. The late fee accrues immediately upon non-receipt and is not subject to a grace period.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall provide Landlord with a security deposit in the amount of four thousand eight hundred dollars ($4,800.00) — equal to two (2) months rent — at lease execution. The security deposit shall be held by Landlord in any account of Landlord's choosing, and any interest earned shall belong to Landlord. The security deposit is non-refundable in the event of any breach of this Lease by Tenant.

5. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may not terminate this Lease before the end of the term except upon payment of three (3) months rent as an early termination fee, plus all rent owed through the end of the term. Tenant remains liable for the entire balance regardless of whether Landlord re-rents the Premises.

6. SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not sublet, assign, or otherwise transfer any interest in the Premises under any circumstances. Any attempted sublet or assignment shall be a material breach of this Lease and shall result in immediate termination and forfeiture of the security deposit.

7. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and maintenance of the Premises during the lease term, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical, heating, cooling, and appliance repairs costing less than five hundred dollars ($500.00) each. Tenant accepts the Premises AS IS and waives any implied warranty of habitability. Tenant shall not withhold rent or pursue any repair-and-deduct remedy.

8. LANDLORD ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises at any reasonable time, with or without prior notice, for any purpose Landlord deems necessary, including showing the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, performing inspections, or conducting maintenance.

9. PETS. No pets of any kind are permitted on the Premises. This prohibition applies without exception, including any animal claimed as a service or emotional support animal. Violation of this clause shall result in immediate eviction.

10. ATTORNEYS FEES. In any action arising under or related to this Lease, Tenant shall pay all of Landlord's attorneys fees and court costs, regardless of the outcome of the action.

11. INDEMNIFICATION. Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord from any and all claims, liabilities, damages, and expenses arising on or related to the Premises, including but not limited to claims caused by Landlord's own negligent acts or omissions.

12. JURY TRIAL WAIVER. The parties hereby waive their right to a trial by jury in any action arising under or related to this Lease, whether sounding in contract, tort, or under any statute.

13. RETALIATION CLAUSE. Tenant agrees that Landlord may, at Landlord's sole discretion, terminate this Lease and require Tenant to vacate within fifteen (15) days following any complaint by Tenant to a governmental authority concerning the condition of the Premises.

14. PARKING. Tenant is assigned one parking space, designated by Landlord. Landlord may revoke or relocate Tenant parking at any time, for any reason, with no notice and no rent reduction. A parking fee of seventy-five dollars ($75.00) per month shall be added to rent.

15. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey. The parties acknowledge that this Lease has been executed in New Jersey and the Premises are located in New Jersey.

By signing below, Tenant acknowledges receipt of a copy of this Lease and agrees to be bound by all of its terms.

LANDLORD: Hudson Realty LLC, by Aaron Whitfield, Managing Partner

TENANT: Maya Chen
